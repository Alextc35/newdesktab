# Asset provenance

The product logo, product interface and repository-provided artwork used by this audiovisual project originate in the New DeskTab repository by **alextc35**. Source locations are recorded in `RESEARCH.md` and the capture script. The original repository is distributed under the following MIT license notice, preserved verbatim below.

Captured bookmarks are demonstration data. Their labels describe user-supplied website shortcuts; no third-party application UI or proprietary music is included. Bundled category icons from the product avoid a dependency on live third-party favicon downloads. Any appearance artwork authored by the capture script is generated locally and identified in that script.

The instrumental score and sound effects are generated originally for this project. See `public/audio/LICENSE.md` and `scripts/generate-audio.mjs` for their separate provenance and generation details. No stock or commercial music recording is incorporated.

React, Remotion, TypeScript and their dependencies retain their own package licenses. The product's MIT notice does not relicense those tools. Consult the installed packages' license files for their terms.

Editorial typography uses Manrope, Copyright 2018 The Manrope Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The unmodified variable font and full notice are bundled in `public/assets/Manrope.ttf` and `public/assets/Manrope-OFL.txt`. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope. Product screenshots preserve the application's own typography.
